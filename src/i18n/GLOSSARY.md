# Translation glossary (English → Shqip)

Shqip is the default language. Write for cashiers, warehouse staff, sales reps and managers: short, everyday standard Albanian (gjuha standarde), polite "ju" form in instructions, verbs in the imperative for buttons ("Ruaj", "Shto rresht"). Use the official term where the law or the tax office uses one (TVSH, NIPT, Libri i shitjeve, Fatura tatimore, Raporti Z). Never leave English words in Albanian text unless listed here as kept.

Sentence case in both languages. Keep placeholders `{name}` exactly. Albanian uses the definite form for labels/headers ("Statusi", "Data", "Klienti") and the indefinite form inside sentences.

## Dates and numbers
Formatting follows the language automatically (formatDate, formatMoney, formatMonth, intlLocale()). Albanian: "24 tet 2026", "1 234,50". Never hard-code 'en-GB'.
Relative words: Today Sot · Yesterday Dje · Tomorrow Nesër · This week Këtë javë · This month Këtë muaj · Last month Muajin e kaluar · This quarter Këtë tremujor · Year to date Nga fillimi i vitit · This year Këtë vit.
